Boosting Drive with Tongkat Ali: The Truth and Your Action Plan

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Tongkat Ali may modestly boost libido, energy, and free testosterone in some older men with low levels, but it is not a miracle cure and does not replace prescription therapy.

A smart plan starts with lab work (total and free testosterone, SHBG, LH/FSH, thyroid, blood sugar) and optimizing sleep, resistance training, weight, diet, stress, and alcohol intake. From there, a standardized extract taken early in the day for 4 to 8 weeks may help, while you monitor sleep, mood, and side effects. Important safety cautions apply: men with heart disease, hormone-sensitive cancers, or those taking interacting medications should avoid it.

Because symptoms like low libido, fatigue, and mood changes can stem from many causes beyond low testosterone, including thyroid issues, blood sugar problems, or sleep disorders, it's worth clarifying what's actually driving how you feel before starting any supplement. What Is Tongkat Ali?

Tongkat Ali (Eurycoma longifolia) is a plant native to Southeast Asia. It has been used traditionally to support:

Male sexual health

Energy levels

Fertility

Physical performance

Today, it's marketed as a natural testosterone booster and libido enhancer.

But marketing is not medicine. So what does research show?

How Tongkat Ali May Work

Tongkat Ali appears to influence the body in a few key ways:

May help increase free testosterone by reducing sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG), which ties up testosterone.

May stimulate natural testosterone production in men with low levels.

May reduce cortisol (stress hormone), which can suppress testosterone when elevated long-term.

May improve sperm quality and motility in some men.

Importantly, Tongkat Ali does not act like synthetic testosterone. It doesn't replace your body's hormone. Instead, it may help your body optimize what it already produces — especially if levels are low.

Does Tongkat Ali Work for Seniors?

This is the critical question.

Short Answer:

It may help some older men — particularly those with low testosterone — but it is not a miracle fix.

What Research Suggests

Clinical studies in middle-aged and older men have shown:

Increased testosterone levels in men with low baseline levels

Improved libido in some participants

Improved mood and reduced fatigue

Better stress hormone balance

Some improvement in muscle strength and body composition

One important pattern: Men with low testosterone tend to see more benefit than men with normal levels.

If your testosterone is already normal for your age, Tongkat Ali is less likely to produce dramatic changes.

Why Testosterone Matters in Seniors

Testosterone naturally declines with age. After age 30, levels drop about 1% per year.

Symptoms of low testosterone (also called Late-Onset Hypogonadism) may include:

Reduced sex drive

Erectile difficulties

Low energy

Loss of muscle mass

Increased body fat

Depressed mood

Poor concentration

Decreased bone density

However, these symptoms overlap with many other conditions — including poor sleep, stress, thyroid issues, depression, and chronic illness.

Is Tongkat Ali Safe for Seniors?

Generally, studies suggest Tongkat Ali is well tolerated when taken at appropriate doses.

Common mild side effects may include:

Restlessness

Insomnia (if taken late in the day)

Irritability

Mild digestive upset

Higher doses may increase heart rate or cause sleep disruption.

Seniors should be especially cautious if they:

Have heart disease

Take blood pressure medication

Take diabetes medication

Are on testosterone therapy

Have hormone-sensitive cancers (such as prostate cancer)

Even natural supplements can interact with medications.

Always speak to a doctor before starting Tongkat Ali — especially if you have a chronic medical condition.
