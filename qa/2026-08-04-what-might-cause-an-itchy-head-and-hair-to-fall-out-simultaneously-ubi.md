Explanation

Having an itchy head and losing hair at the same time can be concerning. One possible cause is a fungal infection. Another possible cause is scalp dysesthesia, which is a condition where the nerves in the scalp are irritated, leading to itching and discomfort. This irritation can sometimes result in hair loss because the scalp is not healthy.

[hair to fall](https://peplawllc.com/)

Another potential cause is autoimmune diseases. These are conditions where the body’s immune system mistakenly attacks its own tissues, including hair follicles. This attack can lead to hair falling out as the follicles are damaged. Some autoimmune diseases specifically target hair growth, causing both itching and hair loss.

If you are experiencing these symptoms, it might be helpful to talk to a healthcare provider who can help determine the specific cause and suggest appropriate treatments.
