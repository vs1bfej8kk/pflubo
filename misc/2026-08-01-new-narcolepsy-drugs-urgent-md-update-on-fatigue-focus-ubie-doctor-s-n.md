Do new narcolepsy drugs improve fatigue and cognitive function?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

New medications for narcolepsy, such as modafinil, are proven to reduce excessive daytime sleepiness and improve cognitive function, including focus, memory, and mental clarity. Modafinil works by promoting wakefulness in the brain, making it a widely prescribed option for people managing narcolepsy symptoms.

However, persistent fatigue and brain fog aren't always caused by narcolepsy—conditions like sleep apnea, thyroid disorders, anemia, or depression can produce similar symptoms. Identifying the true cause is essential for effective treatment.
