# Armpit Muscle Pain? Don't Ignore These Warning Signs Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/14/2026

What could cause pain in the armpit muscle?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Armpit muscle pain is most often caused by muscle strain, overuse, or inflammation from activities like heavy lifting, repetitive arm movements, or new workouts. In some cases, swollen lymph nodes, pinched nerves, or referred pain from the shoulder or chest can also be responsible. Identifying the exact cause matters, because treatment varies widely—from simple rest and stretching to medical evaluation for infections or nerve issues.

Explanation

Your armpit muscle pain might be caused by a few things. One common reason is muscle strain. This can happen when you use your arm a lot or if you have an injury while exercising. The muscle gets tired or tiny tears happen, causing pain. Sometimes, pain can also come along with swollen lymph nodes, which means your immune system is fighting off an infection. This type of pain, known as myalgia with lymphadenopathy, can make the area sore. There are also rare cases where pain in the armpit may be connected to nerve irritation. One study showed that persistent arm pain after surgery or injury could come from nerve issues in the area. In other words, the pain might not just be from the muscle itself but from nearby tissues like lymph nodes or nerves.
