Are flavanols safe to take every day?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Flavanols, natural compounds found in cocoa, tea, apples, and berries, are generally safe to consume daily for most people. Research suggests daily flavanol intake may support heart health by improving blood flow, lowering blood pressure, and enhancing circulation.

However, individual responses vary. Factors like existing health conditions, medications, or unexplained symptoms can influence how your body reacts to dietary changes.
