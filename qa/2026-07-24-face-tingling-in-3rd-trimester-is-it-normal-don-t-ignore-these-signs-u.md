Is face tingling common during the third trimester of pregnancy?

Internal Medicine

Face tingling during pregnancy is uncommon, but it can occur. One possible cause is Bell's palsy, a temporary facial nerve condition that pregnant women—especially in the third trimester—are more likely to develop. Other potential causes include nerve compression, vitamin deficiencies (such as B12), swelling that presses on nerves, or circulation changes related to pregnancy.

Because facial tingling can occasionally signal a more serious condition, it's important to identify the underlying cause quickly. During pregnancy, especially in the third trimester, some women might experience facial nerve issues, like Bell's palsy, which can lead to symptoms such as face tingling. This condition involves sudden weakness or paralysis of the muscles on one side of the face.
