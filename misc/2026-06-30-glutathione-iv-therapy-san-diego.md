What glutathione is and why it matters

Glutathione is a small molecule your body makes from three amino acids. It is the most abundant antioxidant produced inside your cells, and it supports the body’s natural detoxification processes and immune function. Because it is so central to how cells manage oxidative stress, it has been studied widely.

What the research on glutathione shows

Human studies have looked mostly at oral glutathione. One six-month randomized trial found that daily oral glutathione raised body stores of glutathione in healthy adults [1]. Other controlled research has been more mixed, with some trials finding little change in certain oxidative-stress markers [2].

IV delivery is used in clinics because it bypasses digestion, though controlled data specific to IV glutathione is more limited. The takeaway is that glutathione biology is well understood, while the clinical picture for supplementation is still developing.
