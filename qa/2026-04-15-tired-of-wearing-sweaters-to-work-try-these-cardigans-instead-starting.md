What to Consider

Fit

Slim or tailored cuts highlight your frame and layer neatly under jackets or blazers. Relaxed fits give room for layering over t-shirts and button-downs.

Material

Cotton

Lightweight, breathable, and easy to care for. Great for year-round wear, but it can stretch out over time and isn’t as warm as wool.

Polyester

Durable, often cheaper, and resistant to wrinkling, but it doesn’t breathe as well and can feel synthetic against the skin.

Wool

Holds its shape, provides warmth, and naturally regulates temperature. Wool is ideal for cooler months and gives a more polished, premium look than cotton or synthetics.

Cashmere

Super soft and luxurious, with excellent warmth-to-weight ratio. It drapes beautifully and feels high-end, but it requires more care and is prone to pilling if not treated gently.

Texture and Design

Cable, bouclé, or ribbed knits add visual interest and can elevate your outfit without extra layers.Smooth knits or fine-gauge weaves are more versatile and easier to dress up or down. Patterns and color—like subtle checks or tonal stripes—can give a cardigan personality but work best when the rest of your outfit is simple.

Durability and Care

Look for reinforced cuffs, hems, and button plackets to prevent stretching or fraying.Machine-washable fabrics or blends with polyester are easier for regular wear. Consider how often you plan to wear it—heavier wools and structured knits often last longer but need more maintenance.
