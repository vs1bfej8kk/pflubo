How does ovulation affect mood swings?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ovulation can trigger mood swings because of the hormonal shifts that occur during this phase of the menstrual cycle. As estrogen peaks and then drops sharply, and testosterone briefly rises, some people experience irritability, anxiety, or emotional highs and lows. However, the intensity and type of mood changes vary significantly from person to person, depending on hormone sensitivity, overall health, and lifestyle factors.

Explanation

Ovulation is a part of the menstrual cycle when an egg is released from the ovary. This process is controlled by hormones, which can also affect mood.

Hormonal Changes:

During ovulation, levels of certain hormones, such as estrogen and progesterone, change. These changes can influence mood, making some people feel more emotional or irritable.
