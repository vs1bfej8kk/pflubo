# PCOS Hair Loss & Creatine: Stop Before You Supplement | MD Note | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/14/2026

Is there a link between creatine supplementation and hair loss in individuals with PCOS?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

There is no strong evidence linking creatine supplementation to hair loss, even in those with PCOS.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

Creatine is a popular supplement often used to improve athletic performance. Concerns about creatine causing hair loss have arisen, but the current scientific evidence does not support a direct link between creatine use and hair loss.

Research Overview: Studies have investigated the relationship between creatine and hair loss, primarily focusing on how creatine might affect hormone levels. However, these studies do not provide clear evidence that creatine causes hair loss.

[You Supplement MD](https://bioreg.wiki/8tcy9fmflw/)

PCOS Consideration: Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) involves hormonal imbalances, which can sometimes lead to hair loss. However, there is no specific evidence from the provided references that creatine exacerbates hair loss in individuals with PCOS.

In conclusion, while creatine is widely used and studied, current research does not establish a direct connection between creatine supplementation and hair loss.
