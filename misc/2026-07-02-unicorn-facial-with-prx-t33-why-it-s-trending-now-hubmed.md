# Unicorn Facial with PRX‑T33: Why It’s Trending Now | Hubmed

The Unicorn Facial, also known as PRX‑T33 biorevitalization, has gained rapid popularity among injectors and aestheticians for its ability to deliver striking skin rejuvenation without downtime. Combining a unique application of trichloroacetic acid (TCA), hydrogen peroxide, and kojic acid, this treatment stimulates collagen and elastin deep in the dermis, without causing peeling or disrupting the epidermis. That’s why many industry professionals refer to it as a “peel that doesn’t peel”.

Unlike standard chemical peels, which physically exfoliate and can leave skin red, flaky, and photosensitive for days, PRX‑T33 is applied with a specific massage technique that energizes deeper layers while maintaining epidermal integrity. As a result, patients enjoy firm, bright, and smoother skin with very little visible irritation.

Why PRX-T33 Is Preferable to Traditional Chemical Peels

Though it uses TCA, PRX‑T33 is fundamentally different from conventional peels. Its formulation balances collagen‑stimulating strength with gentleness. Hydrogen peroxide modulates the acid’s activity, minimizing surface damage. Kojic acid adds brightening benefits and particularly helps with pigmentation concerns. The combination supports fibroblast activity without triggering visible shedding or prolonged downtime.

In contrast, chemical peels vary widely, from mild to deep, and often come with several days of recovery, increased photosensitivity, and risk of post‑treatment hyperpigmentation, especially in darker skin types. PRX‑T33 is non‑photosensitizing and safe year‑round across pigmentation levels, making it highly reliable for aesthetic facial harmony.
