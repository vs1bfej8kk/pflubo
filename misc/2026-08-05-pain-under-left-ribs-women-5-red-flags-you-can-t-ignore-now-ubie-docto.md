What does pain on the left side under the ribs mean for women?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Left-side pain under the ribs in women is often caused by twelfth rib syndrome or slipping rib syndrome, conditions where the lower ribs shift or move abnormally, irritating nearby nerves and tissues. This can lead to sharp, aching, or intermittent pain that worsens with movement, bending, or deep breathing. Other possible causes include muscle strain, digestive issues, or gynecological conditions, making accurate identification essential.

Explanation

Pain under the ribs on the left side in women can be due to a few conditions. One possibility is twelfth rib syndrome, where the last rib moves and causes pain. This condition can be uncomfortable and may feel like a sharp or dull ache.

Another condition is slipping rib syndrome, which happens when the ribs move out of place. This movement can lead to pain in the area under the ribs, and it can be especially noticeable when bending or twisting the body.

Both of these conditions involve the ribs moving more than they should, which can lead to discomfort.
