# Stop Pain Now: Does Bariatric Surgery Relieve Chronic Pain? | Ubie Doctor's Note

Does bariatric surgery relieve chronic pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Bariatric surgery can reduce chronic pain in many patients, particularly joint and back pain linked to excess weight. However, results vary. Some people continue to experience pain, require ongoing pain medication, or develop new discomfort after surgery. Factors like the type of procedure, pre-existing conditions, and post-op lifestyle all influence outcomes.

Explanation

Bariatric surgery can help some people lose weight, which might reduce chronic pain. However, it doesn't always make pain go away completely.

Pain Medication Use: Some people take less pain medication after surgery, but not everyone. This means that while some see improvements, others might still need medication to manage pain.

Weight Loss and Pain: Losing weight can help with pain, but keeping the weight off is important too. If someone gains the weight back, the pain might return or get worse.

Chronic Pain and Opioid Use: For some, surgery might not change their pain levels, and they might continue using pain medications, including opioids, after surgery. This is important to consider if someone is thinking about surgery for pain relief.
