Face masks are once again in the spotlight for health reasons, thanks to the surge in the highly contagious Omicron variant and the new CDC mask guidance—to wear one in indoor public spaces and crowded outdoor areas, even if you’ve been vaccinated.

Now that it’s clear that the Omicron variant is not only the predominant strain in the U.S. but is much more contagious than previous coronavirus forms including Delta, many people are wondering if their mask is up to the task of protecting them.

Do you need to upgrade your mask now? Here’s the latest on what has and hasn’t changed, and why a good mask is so important now.

First of all, the particles and the size of the virus hasn’t changed. So your mask doesn’t need to be filtering out a different size and type of virus particle.

Compared to Delta, the Omicron variant multiplies quicker within respiratory particles from your nose and throat, making transmissibility even higher, Eric Asher, M.D., Family Medicine Physician at Lenox Hill Hospital, explained by email. "This is why a high quality mask is so important."

Why cloth masks no longer make the cut

"Cloth masks do not protect against smaller air particles that can cause COVID, which is why they are not preferred," Dr. Ascher explained by email. Since Omicron has mutated to be more infectious than Delta, a higher level of filtration is needed, and cloth masks simply don't do enough.
