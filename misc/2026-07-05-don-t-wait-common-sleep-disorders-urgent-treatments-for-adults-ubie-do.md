Common sleep disorders in adults include insomnia, sleep apnea, restless legs syndrome, and narcolepsy. Symptoms range from difficulty falling asleep and loud snoring to daytime fatigue and leg discomfort at night. Treatment options include lifestyle changes, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT-I), CPAP devices, and medications. Because sleep issues often overlap with other health conditions, identifying the root cause is key to effective treatment. Explanation

Adults often experience sleep problems like insomnia, sleep apnea, and restless legs syndrome. These disorders can make it hard to get a good night's sleep.

Common Sleep Disorders:

Insomnia: Trouble falling or staying asleep. It can be treated with better sleep habits, therapy, or medication.

Sleep Apnea: Breathing stops and starts during sleep. Using a CPAP machine or losing weight can help.

Restless Legs Syndrome: Uncomfortable feelings in the legs that make you want to move them. It can be managed with lifestyle changes or medication.

[Common Sleep Disorders Urgent](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/2968981549.html)

Treatment Options:

Lifestyle Changes: Going to bed at the same time every night, avoiding caffeine, and creating a relaxing bedtime routine.

Medical Treatments: CPAP machines for sleep apnea, medications for insomnia or restless legs syndrome.
