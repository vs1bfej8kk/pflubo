How can you differentiate between a sprained ankle and a fractured ankle?

[Sprained vs Fractured](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/8050583137.html)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A sprained ankle occurs when the ligaments connecting the bones are stretched or torn, whereas a broken ankle refers to an actual fracture of one or more bones in the joint. Both injuries share common symptoms like pain, swelling, and bruising, making them difficult to distinguish without professional evaluation. However, a fracture typically causes more severe symptoms, including intense pain, visible deformity, and inability to bear weight. Medical imaging, such as an X-ray, is required to confirm a break.

Because the symptoms overlap so significantly, self-diagnosing can lead to delayed treatment and long-term complications. Diagnosis and Treatment:

Distinguishing between a sprain and a fracture can be challenging based on symptoms alone.

Medical imaging, like X-rays, is often needed to confirm a fracture.

Both injuries may require medical attention, but fractures generally need more intensive treatment, possibly including immobilization or surgery.
