What is NES/T and how does it work as a male contraceptive?

NES/T is an investigational male contraceptive gel that combines two hormones—nestorone (a progestin) and testosterone—to safely suppress sperm production and help prevent pregnancy. Applied daily to the shoulders, NES/T works by lowering natural testosterone in the testes, which reduces sperm count, while replacement testosterone maintains normal hormone levels throughout the body.

Explanation

NES/T is a male contraceptive that combines testosterone and a hormone called nestorone into a gel. This gel is applied to the skin.

How It Works:

[Why You Need to](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

Nestorone: This hormone stops the body from making its own testosterone, which is needed for sperm production.

Testosterone: This hormone in the gel helps keep normal male characteristics like muscle and hair growth.

When used together, these hormones lower sperm production to levels that make pregnancy very unlikely. The gel is applied daily to the skin, usually on the shoulders or upper arms. Studies have shown that this method is effective in reducing sperm count, which is essential for contraception.

[It Works Why](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/)

Using NES/T is a non-invasive and reversible method, meaning that when you stop using it, sperm production can return to normal levels. This makes it a promising option for men seeking temporary contraception.
