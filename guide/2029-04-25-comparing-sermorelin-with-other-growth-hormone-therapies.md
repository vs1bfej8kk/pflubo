What are Growth Hormone Therapies?

Human Growth Hormone (hGH) is a naturally-occuring substance produced by the pituitary gland.

It plays a vital role in growth and development throughout childhood, as well as the repair and maintenance of other body systems throughout adulthood. hGH therapies are medical treatments designed to manage conditions related to hGH deficiency, a condition in which the body does not produce enough growth hormone. These therapies aim to regulate hGH levels in the body, relieving symptoms stemming from a deficiency.

What Is Sermorelin?

Sermorelin is a synthetic peptide designed to imitate naturally-occurring Growth Hormone Releasing Hormones (GHRH). GHRH stimulates the pituitary gland to produce hGH. Sermorelin injections help address low hGH levels and combat the symptoms associated with them.

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved Sermorelin as a treatment for growth deficiencies, primarily among children and teens, under the name Geref in 1997 but revoked the approval in 2004. [1] Researchers in recent years have uncovered benefits for wellness concerns in adults, such as weight loss, athletic performance, and recovery. Doctors are now prescribing Sermorelin off-label for patients who are good candidates for treatment.

Overview of Other Growth Hormone Therapies

Exploring diverse growth hormone therapies beyond Sermorelin unveils a range of treatments. From HGH addressing growth disorders to peptides, each serves different medical requirements.

hGH (Human Growth Hormone)

As noted, hGH is a natural hormone formed by the pituitary gland. Therapeutically, synthetic HGH is used to treat growth disorders in children and hormone deficiencies in adults. It can be administered through injections under the guidance of healthcare experts.

Ipamorelin & CJC-1295

Ipamorelin and CJC-1295 are Growth Hormone-Releasing Peptides (GHRPs) that, similarly to Sermorelin, work together to boost the production of hGH. Ipamorelin stimulates the pituitary gland, while CJC-1295 extends the duration of hGH release.1 These peptides are used mostly for bodybuilding and to promote overall well-being. They are administered through injections, and their usage requires medical supervision.

Tesamorelin

Tesamorelin is another synthetic peptide used in hGH therapies. It is FDA-approved for reducing excess fat and tissue in the abdomen of people with HIV who develop lipodystrophy, which causes changes in fat distribution.2 It is not currently recommended for weight loss or other wellness concerns. Like other growth hormone therapies, Tesamorelin is administered through injections and requires medical oversight.

GHRP-6 & GHRP-2

These peptides are under investigation for their potential to treat growth hormone deficiencies, promote muscle growth, and aid in weight loss. Administration is typically given through injections, with medical professionals closely monitoring the treatment. [1][2]
