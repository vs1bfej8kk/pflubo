Explanation

Sciatica is a condition where you feel pain along the path of the sciatic nerve. This nerve starts in your lower back and travels down through your hips, buttocks, and each leg. People with sciatica often feel pain in their lower back, buttocks, and legs, which can be sharp or burning. Sometimes, it might feel like a jolt or electric shock.

Causes

Herniated Disk: One common cause is a herniated disk in the spine that presses on the nerve.

Bone Spurs: Bone spurs, which are bony growths, can also press on the nerve.

Symptoms

Pain in the back, buttocks, or legs

Risk Factors

Age: As people get older, their spine changes, which can lead to sciatica.

Weight: Being overweight can put more pressure on the spine.

Lifestyle: Sitting for long periods or not getting enough exercise can increase the risk.

Prevention

Stay active and exercise regularly.

Maintain a healthy weight.

Practice good posture when sitting or standing.
