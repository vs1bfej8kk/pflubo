Nike Tech Boreas Hoodie

The Swoosh is not done reinventing the hoodie. Its Tech Boreas sweat is made with a heavyweight fleece that keeps you warm, plus water-repellent panels for gnarly weather. A built-in hood gaiter adds extra wind protection, and zip pockets lock down your stuff, and the oversized fit makes it easy to throw on over anything.

adidas Humanrace Adizero EVO SL by Pharrell Williams

[Style Editors Found the](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/blog/6147406077.html)

Music legend and Louis Vuitton creative director Pharrell teamed up with Adidas to level up one of its best-selling sneakers. The Humanrace Adizero EVO SL still has the lightweight build and cushioned foam that make it a workhorse, but Pharrell’s spin gives it a fresh look with bold color blocking and design tweaks you won’t see on the standard version. We even named it our Best Do-Everything Shoe in this year’s Sneaker Awards.

Suitsupply Straight Leg Pants

[to Buy This Month](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/1128647244.html)

Guys in your office are probably still rocking slim-fit dress pants, but big, baggy trousers are back. If you want to look sharp without looking like you raided your dad’s closet, these straight-leg pants from Suitsupply hit the sweet spot. They’re roomier than the slim cuts everyone’s been wearing, but not full-on wide-leg. The dark brown color also gives them an edge that works with sneakers or dress shoes.

Bandit RoccaRip™ Windbreaker Jacket

Bandit is the running brand everyone’s starting to talk about, and this windbreaker shows why. It’s lightweight enough to keep you moving, durable enough to handle miles in the rain, and packed with zip pockets so you don’t lose your keys. The clean fit means you can wear it on a run or just tossing it over a T-shirt for errands.

Rowing Blazers Cashmere Stripe Sweater

Rowing Blazers built its rep by making old-school prep style cool again. Take this sweater, which has bold green-and-yellow stripes that are a fresh break from navy-and-white basics, while the waffle knit adds texture. Made in luxe cashmere, it looks sharp under a blazer and plays well with jeans.

Canada Goose Lodge Vest Black Label

Think of the Canada Goose Lodge Vest as the upgrade to the Patagonia fleece every guy in finance already owns. This one packs 750-fill down insulation for real warmth, but the ripstop nylon shell keeps it light and easy to wear all day. It’s water-resistant, cut slimmer so it doesn’t balloon out, and comes in sharp Black Label colors that look grown-up instead of bro-y.

J. Press Made-in-USA Wide Wale British Corduroy Pant

I hit a few menswear shows during September Fashion Week in New York, and J. Press stood out. The Manhattan-based label nails Ivy League style in a way guys from state schools would actually wear. These corduroy pants prove it: the relaxed cut is flattering without being sloppy, and the wide-wale fabric adds texture that instantly upgrades even a plain sweatshirt.

Cole Haan Judson Grand Chelsea Boots

I love a good lace-up, but a Chelsea boot is just easier. You slip it on, it looks polished, and you don’t have to think about it. Cole Haan’s Judson Grand Chelsea has the clean look you want for the office, but it’s built with a cushioned footbed and a rubber sole so it’s actually comfortable. Wear it with dress pants or jeans. It’s the kind of boot that works everywhere.

Vince Double-Neck Pima Cotton T-Shirt

Vince makes some of the best tees in the game, and this new double-neck version proves it. The Pima cotton is super soft, the micro-waffle knit adds subtle texture, and the cut is clean enough to wear solo or under a blazer. It’s one of those tees I put on and instantly feel sharper.

Sporty & Rich x '47 Brand New York Yankees Clean Up

Everyone knows ’47 as the hat brand behind the Yankees caps you see everywhere, and this collab with Sporty & Rich makes it even cooler. The cotton twill is garment-washed for a broken-in feel, and the relaxed fit makes it easy to throw on. Add in the clean embroidery and side branding, and you’ve got a Yankees cap that looks classic but feels fresh.

Buck Mason X Lee Loomstate Selvedge 101B Jean

Buck Mason is one of our favorite new all-American brands, and now they’ve linked with Lee, the OG denim heavyweight. These 101B jeans are built from selvedge denim with a relaxed cut that feels lived-in. They’re rugged, well-made, and go with everything from a hoodie to a blazer.

Reiss Suede Zip-Through Bomber Jacket

Reiss is a British label that just feels sharper than a lot of the mall brands you see in the States, but the prices aren’t worlds apart. This suede bomber proves the point. The cut is sleek, the suede feels premium, and it’s the kind of jacket that’ll still look good years from now. Toss it on with jeans or over a button-down, and it instantly makes everything else you’re wearing look cooler.

Sunspel Classic Boxer Shorts

Sunspel just relaunched the boxers that put the brand on the map. They kept the easy, relaxed fit but updated the fabric with Tencel, a silky-soft fiber that feels great all day. The stripes are what make these stand out: bold vertical lines in retro colors that look like old-school sports gear.

Mack Weldon Ace Half-Zip Sweater

The Mack Weldon Ace Half-Zip is your new go-to layer. The micro-brushed French terry feels soft and broken-in, but the cut stays clean thanks to ribbed cuffs and hem. A hidden zipper pocket adds utility, and flatlock seams keep it sleek.

Shinola Canfield Speedway Open Road Automatic Chronograph Watch

Shinola’s Canfield Speedway Open Road is a 44 mm chronograph with racing DNA. Steel case, bold pushers, and a Swiss automatic movement you can see through the back. It’s sporty but still sharp enough for the office.

Aimé Leon Dore x The North Face Casentino Nuptse Jacket
