Bomber jackets have made great strides in the past few decades, transcending their utilitarian roots to become a closet staple. Like a solid fleece jacket or leather jacket, a good bomber jacket will provide layering potential and optimal warmth. What's also true: The best bomber jackets come in a number of materials (not just black), complement your physique, and add an instant edge to your outfit.

What to Consider

Materials

First and foremost, the best bomber jackets are made of high-quality materials. From suede and nylon to wool and leather, men's bomber jackets can come in several different materials.

Leather

This gives that classic, rugged look and has longevity, but it’s heavier and warmer.

Suede

It’s softer with a luxe look but requires a bit more care. It can easily show marks or scratches if you’re not careful.

Nylon-Polyester Blends

These options are lightweight, weather-resistant, and ideal for everyday wear, while cotton and wool offer breathability and warmth.

Fit and Length

Traditionally, bombers have a shorter cut that hits at the waist, creating a defined silhouette. Make sure it fits your frame comfortably, with enough room for layering but not so loose that it loses shape. Consider if you want a standard or relaxed fit, depending on whether you’re going for a more tailored or casual vibe.

Insulation

If you’re buying for the colder months, look for options with added insulation, like Primaloft or a quilted lining, to keep you warm. Lightweight, unlined bombers are great for layering in milder climates.
