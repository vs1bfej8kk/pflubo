What methods can help manage restless leg syndrome during pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Restless leg syndrome (RLS) during pregnancy is a common condition that causes uncomfortable sensations and an urge to move the legs, especially at night. Management typically includes:

Lifestyle changes: regular gentle exercise, stretching, warm baths, and consistent sleep routines

Iron and folate supplementation: low iron levels are strongly linked to RLS in pregnancy

Avoiding triggers: such as caffeine, nicotine, and prolonged inactivity

Medications: only in severe cases and only under a doctor's guidance, as many RLS drugs are not safe during pregnancy

Because RLS symptoms can overlap with other pregnancy-related conditions like leg cramps, circulation issues, or nerve problems, identifying the true cause is essential to finding effective relief. Lifestyle Changes:

Regular exercise can help, but avoid heavy exercise close to bedtime.
