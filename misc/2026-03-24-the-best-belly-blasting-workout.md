# The Best Belly-Blasting Workout

Between work deadlines, family commitments, and filling in March Madness brackets, there are plenty of reasons to skip the gym. But when it comes to building your best body, time shouldn’t be a limiting factor. That's because you can blast fat and build strength by increasing your workout density—or the amount of work you do in a given length of time—not duration.

"Performing burpees at breakneck speed are a great way to cut workout time without cutting results," says Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., author of The IMPACT! Body Plan. With one exercise, you'll get your heart pumping, activate all your muscles from head to toes, and increase the levels of muscle-building and fat-burning hormones released. The result: a stronger and leaner body in no time.

Here's how to do it: Stand with your feet slightly wider then shoulder-width apart and your arms at your sides. Push your hips back, bend your knees, and lower your body into a squat until you can place your hands on the floor. Kick your legs back into a pushup position. Perform a pushup, and then quickly bring your legs back into the squat position. Stand up quickly. That's 1 rep. Do 25 as fast as possible.
