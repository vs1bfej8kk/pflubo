# James Gandolfini's Son Has the Perfect Role in the 'Sopranos' Prequel

Michael Gandolfini, son of the late Sopranos star, is entering the family business.

According to Deadline, the 19-year-old actor has signed on to play young Tony Soprano—the iconic crime boss his father, James Gandolfini, made famous—in the upcoming Sopranos prequel film, The Many Saints of Newark.

“It’s a profound honor to continue my dad’s legacy while stepping into the shoes of a young Tony Soprano,” the younger Gandolfini told the outlet. “I’m thrilled that I’m going to have the opportunity to work with David Chase and the incredible company of talent he has assembled for The Many Saints of Newark.”

The film, written by series creator Chase, will be set in the 1960s during the Newark race riots, which figured into a flashback in one of the classic series’ first season episodes, and will be a kind of origin story for Tony Soprano. Alan Taylor, who directed seven episodes of the groundbreaking show, is slated to helm the prequel.

News of Michael Gandolfini’s casting comes on the heels of the 20th anniversary of the show’s premier, and nearly six years after James Gandolfini’s death at age 51.

The late actor, who died in 2013, was widely celebrated for the role as the New Jersey gangster, earning three Emmys and a Golden Globe for his performances. The show itself, of course, is considered one of the greatest television programs of all time, and ushered in the golden age of TV. Shows like Breaking Bad and Mad Men, now classics in their own right, bear the Sopranos influence, and likely wouldn’t have been possible had the iconic HBO series not paved the way.
