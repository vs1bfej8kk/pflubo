First: What Is ED and Why Does It Matter?

Erectile dysfunction is the consistent difficulty getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex. Occasional issues are normal. But if it happens regularly, it could signal:

Blood vessel problems

Diabetes

High blood pressure

Hormonal imbalances

Stress, anxiety, or depression

Side effects from medications

ED can sometimes be an early warning sign of heart disease. That's why it's important not to ignore it.

Important Health Considerations

ED medications are not safe for everyone.

You should not take sildenafil or tadalafil if you:

Take nitrates for chest pain

Have severe heart disease

Have uncontrolled low blood pressure

Have had a recent stroke or heart attack

This is why honesty during your online medical intake is critical. These drugs affect blood vessels. Mixing them with certain heart medications can cause dangerous drops in blood pressure.

If you have chest pain, shortness of breath, or symptoms of heart disease, speak to a doctor immediately rather than starting ED medication online.
