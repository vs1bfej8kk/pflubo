At what point should I be concerned about excessive hair loss?

Reviewed by Suo Lee, MD

Internal Medicine

You should be concerned about excessive hair loss if you notice significant thinning, bald patches, or if it is sudden and accompanied by other symptoms. Consult a healthcare professional to determine the cause.

Explanation

Hair loss can be worrying, but understanding when it becomes a concern is important. If you notice a lot of hair falling out, thinning, or bald spots, it may be time to pay attention. Sudden hair loss or hair loss with other symptoms like itching or redness should be checked out.

Possible Causes:

Nutritional Factors: Poor nutrition can lead to hair loss. Eating a balanced diet helps keep your hair healthy.
