# Mark Wahlberg Paused His Infamous Morning Routine for Quarantine

Actor and fitness buff Mark Wahlberg is famous for many reasons—over two decades of box office successes chief among them—but recently, he's been renowned for a ridiculously early wake up time of 2:30 a.m. to guarantee he has time to dedicate to his workouts with his busy work and family life.

When Wahlberg dropped the aspirational routine in an Instagram story, it broke the fitness and celebrity-focused internet. The full program has been emulated time and time again by YouTubers and bloggers for millions of views. Joe Rogan called Wahlberg a "legit stud" on a recent episode of his infamous podcast, saying, “When you look at his dedication to fitness, he’s not bullshitting. He is really up at 5 o’clock in the morning working out like a beast every day.”

Wahlberg has continued to be diligent about keeping up his workouts during quarantine, sharing multiple posts and even hosting a session on Instagram for his fans. But just because he's still working hard doesn't mean he's working in the same ways as before.

Wahlberg revealed in his new Men's Health cover story interview that during the quarantine period, his wake up time was closer to 9 a.m.—and he hit the gym at 10:30. The guy really is human.

The actor shared that he begins every day by eating a vegetarian breakfast—typically plant-based sausage with a sweet-potato patty and avocado accompanied by juice shots—before descending to his basement home gym by 10:30.

“That’s usually like my midafternoon lunchtime,” he says. “I’m still training five days a week, but now my wife is with me. I feel like I’m a human alarm clock. I’m pushing her to get down there. . . The rest of the day, I’m eating more and drinking more wine than I normally would.”

Wahlberg says that his schedule won't stay like this forever, though. “I’m just maintaining until I get the word that we might be going back to work. Then I’ll get super disciplined again."
