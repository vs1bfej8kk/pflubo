Frequent numbness in the back is often a sign of nerve compression, irritation, or an underlying spinal condition. Common causes include herniated discs, pinched nerves, poor posture, or, less commonly, more serious neurological issues. Because nerve-related symptoms can worsen without proper care, a timely medical evaluation is essential to identify the root cause and guide effective treatment.
