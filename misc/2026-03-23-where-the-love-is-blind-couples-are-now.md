# Where the 'Love Is Blind' Couples Are Now

The "Pod Squad" are back, baby!

The fourth season of Love Is Blind may have ended earlier this year on Netflix with a special live reunion episode, but the fun doesn't stop there. Love Is Blind: After the Altar will catch up with this year's couples to see just how blissful married life is turning out to be after they said "I do" at the end of the season.

But as Shakespeare himself wrote, the course of true love never did run smooth—and let's be honest, that is doubly true when you're talking about reality TV. So it shouldn't surprise anybody to learn that life after Love Is Blind proved troublesome for some of the couples. Here's everything we know about where the Season 4 cast are now, and which couples are still together.

Paul and Micah

Despite originally expressing interest in other people, Paul and Micah eventually formed a connection and ended up walking down the aisle... but not without their misgivings. Micah was so reluctant to make her vows that she let Paul go first, and he told her and everyone present: "I don't."

Ultimately, Paul revealed in a confessional that he couldn't ever imagine marrying Micah, or having children with her—a revelation which Micah felt was "the worst thing" he could have said to her.

Paul has since confirmed that he and Micah did briefly reunite after their defunct wedding ceremony, but that as of right now, they are definitely not together.

Marshall and Jackie

Jackie agreed to get engaged to Marshall even though she still had feelings for another Season 4 contestant, Josh, leading to tension between her and her new fiancé. And when Josh contacted her to say that her feelings were reciprocated, Jackie skipped her wedding dress fitting to go on a coffee date with him.

Or at least, that's the timeline that the Love Is Blind editing team presented. Jackie insists that it unfolded differently. "I broke up with Marshall before I saw Josh at the coffee shop," she told host Vanessa Lachey. "It looks like I'm a cheater, but I'm not a cheater."

Also contributing to the drama was Jackie's claim that Marshall made some pretty derogatory remarks about her appearance while they were signing their marriage license, including a transphobic slur, which he denies.

Jackie is now dating Josh.

Brett and Tiffany

After the tumultuous drama of Season 4, this couple will restore your faith in love. Brett and Tiffany seemed made for each other from day one, and the only conflict that seemed to arise on their wedding day arose from the fact that Brett's suit didn't fit perfectly, as he wanted everything to be just perfect for the special occasion. Brett and Tiffany are now living together in Portland.

Zack and Bliss

Zack was originally engaged to the "mean girl" of the season, Irina, but their relationship fizzled out during a vacation to Mexico, during which Irina admitted that she was attracted to Paul. Zack then got back in touch with Bliss, whose company he had enjoyed in the pods, eventually proposing to her.

Kwame and Chelsea

This couple faced several obstacles on their way to the altar, from a disagreement over whether they would live once they were married, to Chelsea struggling to gain the approval of Kwame's mother. Finally, though, the big day arrived, with Kwame's family present, and the couple got hitched.

But that wasn't the end of the couple's issues: Chelsea still felt some kind of way about all of Kwame's flirting with Micah during the shared couples' trip to Mexico. Kwame apologized, calling his actions "immature" and the result of "ego."

"We were strong," Chelsea said, "but if anything, this whole thing made us even stronger."
