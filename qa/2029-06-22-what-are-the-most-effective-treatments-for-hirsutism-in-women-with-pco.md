What are the most effective treatments for hirsutism in women with PCOS?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Hirsutism in women with PCOS is most effectively treated through a combination of medications and lifestyle changes. Oral contraceptives help regulate hormones, while spironolactone blocks androgens that cause excess hair growth. Weight management, a balanced diet, and regular exercise further improve hormonal balance and reduce symptoms. In some cases, topical treatments like eflornithine cream or hair removal procedures (laser, electrolysis) offer additional relief. A tailored approach, guided by a healthcare provider, delivers the best long-term results.
