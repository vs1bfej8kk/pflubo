Is it common to have irregular periods after giving birth?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Postpartum irregular periods are common and typically normal. After giving birth, your body undergoes significant hormonal shifts as it transitions out of pregnancy, which can cause cycles to be unpredictable in timing, flow, and duration. Breastfeeding can further delay or alter menstruation due to elevated prolactin levels, which suppress ovulation. Most people see their cycles regulate within a few months, though this varies widely.

That said, "irregular" can mean many things — and while postpartum changes are usually harmless, symptoms like heavy bleeding, severe pain, or prolonged absence of periods may signal an underlying issue worth checking.
