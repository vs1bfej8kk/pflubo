What is Burn/Scald of Roof of Mouth?

Palatal thermal injury or palatal burn happens when the roof of the mouth (palate) gets damaged from coming into contact with heat or hot substances.

Treatment of Burn/Scald of Roof of Mouth

[of Roof](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/3970821974.html)

Treatment for burns or scalds of the roof of the mouth includes pain management, maintaining hydration, and a soft diet to avoid further irritation. In severe cases, surgical intervention may be needed to address significant tissue damage.
