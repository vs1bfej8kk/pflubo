# Jeremy Renner Remembers His 'Skull Crushing' During His Accident

ON A SNOWY morning in January of 2023, actor Jeremy Renner was clearing snow out of the driveway of his Nevada home with a 14,000-pound Pisten-Bully snow groomer. He hopped out of the cab quickly to talk to his nephew—and, as he did, the groomer rolled forward. As Renner tried to hop back into the cab to stop it, his foot got pulled under, and dragged his body underneath the massive machine.

"It undulates, because there’s four sections of tires with these metal tracks—it pulls it like a tank," Renner tells Men's Health in a new cover story. "I remember every undulation. I remember my head cracking on the thing and it just pressing on me—it’s exactly like you think it would feel. An immovable object and a crushing force, and something’s gotta give. But thank God my skull didn’t fully give."

Of course, it had to give some: Renner suffered a broken cheekbone and eye socket—to the point that his left eyeball bulged out of his head. Those were just a few of the 38-plus broken bones suffered. He was airlifted to the hospital where he spent 12 days in intensive care.
