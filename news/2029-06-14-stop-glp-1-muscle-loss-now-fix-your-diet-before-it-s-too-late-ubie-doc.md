How can diet prevent muscle loss on GLP-1s?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Protein intake plays a critical role for people using GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, or Mounjaro. Because these drugs reduce appetite and often lead to rapid weight loss, users can lose muscle mass alongside fat. Eating more protein—typically 1.2 to 1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight daily—helps preserve lean muscle, supports metabolism, and improves overall body composition during treatment.
