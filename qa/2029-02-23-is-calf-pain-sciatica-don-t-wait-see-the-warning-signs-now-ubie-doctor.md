Calf pain is not typically listed as a hallmark symptom of sciatica. Sciatica most commonly causes pain, tingling, numbness, or weakness that radiates from the lower back through the buttock and down the back of the leg. While some people with sciatica may feel discomfort extending into the calf, calf pain on its own is more often linked to other conditions, such as muscle strain, cramps, poor circulation, or in more serious cases, a blood clot (deep vein thrombosis).

Because calf pain can have many possible causes—some minor and others urgent—it's important to understand what's really behind your symptoms.
