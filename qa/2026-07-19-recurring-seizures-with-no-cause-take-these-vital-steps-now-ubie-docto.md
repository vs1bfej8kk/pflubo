Explanation

When seizures occur again and again without a known cause, it can be confusing and worrying. Even if doctors can't find out why the seizures are happening, there are still steps that can be taken to help.

Talk to a Doctor: It's important to see a healthcare provider who knows about seizures. They can help figure out the best way to manage the seizures.

Understand Risks: The chance of seizures occurring again can vary. Some people may have more seizures, while others may not. Knowing the risk can help in planning care.

Treatment Options: Even without a clear cause, there may be medicines or other treatments that can help control the seizures.
