# How to Appear More Attractive to Women Instantly

In the world of competing to win a right swipe, you might think your photo can make or break your chances.

But even if you’re horribly awkward in front of the camera—or if your mug doesn’t quite measure up to Chris Hemsworth levels—you can still gain her attention. You just need to play up your awesome creativity, a new study published in the journal Royal Society Open Science suggests.

In the study, researchers looked at how creativity might affect a person’s attractiveness. Rather than trolling Tinder, they showed a group of volunteers a library of photos of both men and women, and had them rate each face purely on attractiveness.

With the hot or not scales in place, they then showed the same batch of photos to a second group of volunteers—this time adding a little info to each image that gave clues as to whether the person in the photo might have a cool, creative personality. The volunteers then and rated attractiveness based on their overall impression.

The result? Average-looking dudes become more enticing when women perceived them as more creative. In fact, the effect was so strong that they were rated just as attractive as the most Brad Pitt-esque men in the bunch.
