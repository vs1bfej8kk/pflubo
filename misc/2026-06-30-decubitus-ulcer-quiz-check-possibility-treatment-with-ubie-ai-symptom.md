What is Decubitus Ulcer?

[Treatment with Ubie](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/)

Also known as bedsores, these ulcers form due to extended pressure on skin's pressure points. This condition occurs in patients with prolonged immobility and is common in bedridden patients. Usual pressure points include the lower back and buttocks. Malnourished patients have a higher risk.

Typical Symptoms of Decubitus Ulcer

Welts appear when skin is scratched or rubbed

Bedridden with severe difficulty getting up from lying down

Multiple areas of skin redness or red spots

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Hard or stiff skin

Areas of irritated or peeling skin

Back pain or discomfort

Diagnostic Questions for Decubitus Ulcer

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Does your skin get red from friction or irritation?

Are you bedridden and having severe difficulty getting up from lying down?

Do you have multiple red areas or spots on your skin?

Do you have pus-filled blisters or bumps on your skin?

Are there any skin problems on your stomach, chest, or back?

Treatment of Decubitus Ulcer

Regular turning and special cushions can lower the risk for susceptible patients. Once bedsores occur, they are difficult to treat. They may need special dressings or surgical procedures to aid wound healing. The affected tissue or area is at risk of infection and can lead to hospitalization for antibiotics. Patients may need surgery to help treat infection and promote healing.
