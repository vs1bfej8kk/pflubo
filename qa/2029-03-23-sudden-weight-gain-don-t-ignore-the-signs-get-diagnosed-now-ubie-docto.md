How can unexplained weight gain be diagnosed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Unexplained weight gain is typically diagnosed by reviewing your medical history, lifestyle habits, and screening for underlying conditions such as hypothyroidism, Cushing's syndrome, polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), or side effects from medications like insulin or antidepressants. A doctor may order blood tests, hormone panels, or imaging to identify the root cause.

Because unexplained weight gain can signal a wide range of issues—from hormonal imbalances to metabolic disorders—it's important to identify possible causes early.
