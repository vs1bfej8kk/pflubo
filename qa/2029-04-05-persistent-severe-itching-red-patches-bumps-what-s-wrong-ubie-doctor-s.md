Dr. Taylor’s Response

I'm sorry to hear you're experiencing such intense and persistent itching. This sounds very uncomfortable and distressing. While I can't provide a definitive diagnosis, I can share some information about potential causes of widespread itching:

Allergic reactions (to food, medication, or environmental factors)

Eczema or atopic dermatitis

Psoriasis

Hives (urticaria)

Dry skin (xerosis)

Scabies (mite infestation)

Fungal infections

Thyroid problems

Liver or kidney issues

Certain medications

Given the duration and severity of your symptoms, I strongly recommend seeing a dermatologist as soon as possible. They can perform a thorough examination and possibly run tests to determine the underlying cause.

In the meantime:

Try to avoid scratching, as this can lead to skin damage and infection

Use cool compresses to soothe the skin

Apply unscented, hypoallergenic moisturizer

Take lukewarm (not hot) showers

Wear loose, breathable clothing

Consider using over-the-counter antihistamines (but consult a pharmacist first)

Remember, persistent and severe itching like you're describing warrants professional medical attention. Your comfort and health are important. Take care, and I hope you find relief soon.
