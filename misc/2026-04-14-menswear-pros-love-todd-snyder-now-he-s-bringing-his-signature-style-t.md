# Menswear Pros Love Todd Snyder. Now He's Bringing His Signature Style to Your Living Room

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

TODD SNYDER HAS become one of our favorite menswear brands for its classic, premium tailoring that looks great on most guys—without sky-high price tags. Now he's giving your living room the same treatment. His new 65-piece collaboration with CB2, called the Townhouse Collection, draws inspiration from the warm, collected style of classic New York brownstones. The result: sleek furniture, lighting, barware, and décor designed to look better with age.

SHOP TODD SNYDER’S CB2 COLLECTION

“It's been incredible to explore one of my first passions, interior design, through this collaboration,” Todd Snyder wrote in a press release. “My mother was an artist and interior designer, and she always encouraged my interest in both fashion and interiors. From the start, I channeled these two disciplines in my stores to curate one-of-a-kind spaces. For my first Todd Snyder Store, I shopped for all the furniture myself and enjoyed spending time discovering hidden gems at antique shops and flea markets, ultimately selling them alongside my collections."

If you've ever stepped inside one of Snyder's shops, the collection will feel familiar. It’s full of the same rich textures and tailored finishes that define his clothing, from wool throws and leather catchalls to aged brass lighting, marble-topped furniture, and vintage-inspired barware. Standout pieces include the Tribeca Bar Cabinet, a herringbone-upholstered daybed, and the Marylebone dinnerware collection, all of which bring the same stylish-but-actually-usable vibe that's made Snyder's clothing so popular.

No, it's not budget furniture. But you're getting exactly what you're paying for: quality materials, timeless design, and craftsmanship that's meant to last a lifetime. The Flatiron Daybed tops out at $1,699, but comparable pieces from luxury furniture brands can easily cost three times as much. And if you're not looking to overhaul your apartment, smaller pieces like the porcelain dinnerware, glassware, and leather accessories run well under $100. Shop some of our favorite pieces, below.
