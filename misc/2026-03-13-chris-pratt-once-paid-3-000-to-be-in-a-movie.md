Chris Pratt's acting career happened by accident.

While living in Hawaii and working odd jobs, Pratt was a fairly happy guy.

"It’s a pretty awesome place to be homeless," Pratt told The Independent in 2014. "We just drank and smoked weed and worked minimal hours, just enough to cover gas, food, and fishing supplies."

In a bizarre stroke of luck, he was discovered while waiting tables at the Bubba Gump Shrimp Company in Maui. Actress and director Rae Dawn Chong walked in the door and changed his life by casting him in the 2000 short film Cursed Part 3.

After filming wrapped, Pratt stayed in California and started auditioning. He scored roles on Everwood and The O.C., among other shows, and eventually made it big as the lovable goofball Andy Dwyer on Parks and Recreation.

Before Pratt made it big, he auditioned for a role in Amy Sedaris’ 2003 film, Strangers with Candy. And as he shared with Stephen Colbert some years later, he actually lost money being in that film.

"Did you know that movie cost me $3,000 to be in? This is a testament to how much we love doing what we do that we would pay to do it," Pratt said, adding that in order to stay on budget, the film was only open to actors living in New York City. So, he did the only reasonable thing he could think to do: pay for his flight, pretend to be a local, and put himself up in a hotel.

"I was hungry. I wanted the role," he said.
