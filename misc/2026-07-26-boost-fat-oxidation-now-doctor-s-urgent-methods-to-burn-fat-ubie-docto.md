Explanation

Increasing fat oxidation means helping your body use more fat for energy. This can be done through a combination of exercise and diet.

Exercise: Regular physical activity, especially aerobic exercises like walking, running, or cycling, can boost fat oxidation. Exercise helps train your muscles to use fat more efficiently.

Diet: Eating a balanced diet that includes healthy fats, proteins, and carbohydrates can also support fat oxidation. Some studies suggest that diets lower in carbohydrates may increase the body's ability to burn fat.

Additional Tips:

Consistency: Regular exercise and a consistent diet are key for long-term results.

Intensity: Both low and moderate-intensity exercises can be beneficial, but mixing in some higher-intensity workouts may further enhance fat burning.

By combining these methods, you can help your body become more efficient at using fat for energy, which can support overall health and fitness goals.
