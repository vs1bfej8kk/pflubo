Laser Hair Removal Pre-Treatment Instructions

Proper preparation helps ensure safe treatment and optimal results.

4 Weeks Before Treatment

Avoid sun exposure, tanning beds, and self-tanners

[Pre Post](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/blog/3507943582.html)

Use broad-spectrum SPF 30+ daily on the treatment area

Do not wax, thread, tweeze, or use depilatory creams- This removes the follicle which needs to be in place for treatment.

(Shaving is allowed and should be done night before or the morning of treatment)

1–2 Weeks Before Treatment

Discontinue retinoids, retinol, tretinoin, adapalene

Stop using exfoliating acids (glycolic, salicylic, lactic) and harsh scrubs

Avoid chemical peels, microneedling, IPL, or laser treatments in the area

Notify your provider of any new medications, especially photosensitizing drugs

24–48 Hours Before Treatment

Shave the treatment area (unless otherwise directed)

Avoid alcohol and strenuous exercise

Do not apply lotions, oils, deodorant, or makeup to the area

Day of Treatment

Skin must be clean, dry, untanned, and free of irritation

No makeup, sunscreen, deodorant, or topical products on the treatment area

Inform your provider of:

Recent sun exposure

Pregnancy

Skin infections, open wounds, or rashe

Changes in medications or medical history

Laser Hair Removal Post-Treatment Instructions

(Motus, Aliya, Candela & GentleMax)

Laser hair removal works by targeting the hair follicle to reduce future growth. Proper post-care is important to protect your skin and achieve the best results.

What to Expect:

Mild redness, warmth, or swelling around hair follicles is normal

Sensation may feel like a sunburn

Symptoms typically resolve within a few hours to 24–48 hours

Treated hair may shed over the next 1–3 weeks — this is normal and expected

Post-Treatment Care:

Keep the treated area clean and dry for the remainder of the day

Apply a soothing product (aloe vera or provider-recommended calming cream) as needed

[Post Treatment Care](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Use cool compresses for discomfort or swelling

Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher daily to treated areas exposed to sun

Wear loose, breathable clothing over treated areas

Avoid for 24–48 Hours

[Pre Post](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/)

Hot showers, baths, saunas, steam rooms

Excessive heat or sweating

Strenuous exercise

Tight or abrasive clothing on treated areas

Avoid for 7–14 Days

Sun exposure and tanning beds

Self-tanners on treated areas

Exfoliating products (scrubs, acids, retinoids)

If having trouble with in-grown hair (which will improve as treatments progress) utilizing Dual Action Scrub in between sessions can help

[Post Treatment](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

Waxing, threading, sugaring, or tweezing (shaving is allowed)

Shaving & Hair Regrowth

You may shave as needed between treatments

Do not wax, tweeze, or thread between sessions, as this removes the follicle needed for treatment

Hair shedding may look like regrowth but is part of the normal process

Makeup & Skincare

Makeup may be applied after 24 hours if there is no irritation

Resume gentle skincare once skin has fully calmed

Avoid harsh or active products until redness has resolved

Do Not:

Pick, scratch, or irritate the treated area

Tan before or after treatments

[Post Treatment](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/)

[Pre Post Treatment](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/4168495371.html)

Apply heat to treated skin

[Post Treatment Care](https://healingkinetics.bbsxyxy.com/blog/7956478049.html)

Skip sunscreen — sun exposure increases risk of pigmentation changes

Post Procedure

Area may be mildy red with mild inflammation surrounding the hair follicle. This is normal and will be resolved within a couple hours.

Histamine reactions can occur, if so you may treat with zyrtec or other antihistamine.

You may continue to shave as normal.

When to Contact MD Esthetics

Please contact our office if you experience:

Blistering or crusting

Prolonged swelling or redness beyond 48 hours

Signs of infection

Any reaction that feels unusual or concerning

Treatment Plan & Results

Laser hair removal requires a series of treatments spaced several weeks apart for best results, as hair grows in cycles. Your provider will customize your treatment plan based on your skin type, hair type, and treatment area.
