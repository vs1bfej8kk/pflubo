# Q: How Do I Tackle Oily Skin?

[Tackle Oily Skin](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/)

This week, we asked skin care expert Autumn Henry to answer your question. Autumn is the lead esthetician for Exhale Spa’s flagship location in Midtown Manhattan.

Dear Men’s Health,

[How Do](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/)

I have oily skin and my face is always shiny. What are the do’s and don’ts for taking care of my face?

Mark

Follow @MensHealthStyle on Twitter!

Dear Mark,

This will sound counter-intuitive, but keeping the skin properly hydrated is the best thing to do for oily skin. This means after cleansing, use a water-based serum (iS Clinical's Poly-Vitamin) or toner (Sircuit Skin's Molecular Mist) instead of a heavy moisturizer.

Don't feel bad about your oily skin, though—it'll stave off fine lines and wrinkles in the long-run. For a shine that won't quit, try Dermalogica's Oil Control Lotion. It's an oil-free hydrator that actually absorbs excess oil throughout the day while providing the nutrition your skin needs. I always recommend a gentle exfoliation two or three times a week to decongest the pores of natural build up. Don't scrub too hard, or use harsh chemicals; your skin will just produce more oil to compensate for irritated skin.

—The Men's Health Style Team
